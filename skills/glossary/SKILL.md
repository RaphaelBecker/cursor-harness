# Glossary

Load [`references/glossary.md`](references/glossary.md) when a term is unclear or
when writing plans, contracts, handoffs, or product stories.

## Rules

- Prefer glossary meanings over improvised jargon.
- Keep new terms short and concrete. Add them to the reference file when a durable
  shared meaning is needed.
- Do not paste the whole glossary into every reply — load on demand.
- Product- or stack-specific vocabulary belongs in the consumer project, not this
  portable glossary.
