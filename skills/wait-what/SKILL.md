# Wait, what?

The last message did not land. Re-pitch **that** message only.

1. Use `@glossary` terms. Everyday words. No new jargon.
2. Lead with what changed, why it matters, and what the developer should do
   next.
3. Drop chatbot filler, puffery, and process essays. Keep it short.
